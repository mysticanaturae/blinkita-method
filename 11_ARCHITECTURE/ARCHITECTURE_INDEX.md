
\# Architectural Decision Records™



\## ADR-001



\# Creator First Architecture™



\## Question



Who is the center of the system?



\## Decision



The Creator™ is the primary source of:



\- vision

\- meaning

\- intention

\- responsibility



The system exists to support creators.



\---



\## ADR-002



\# Living World as Primary Entity™



\## Question



What is the fundamental entity of the system?



\## Decision



The primary entity is not the project.



It is the Living World™.



A Living World™ contains:



\- identity

\- experience

\- memory

\- relationships

\- evolution



\---



\## ADR-003



\# Creation Not Project™



\## Question



How should meaningful work be understood?



\## Decision



Projects are implementation tools.



Creations are the enduring entities.



BLINKITA METHOD™ supports evolving creations rather than isolated projects.

\# Architectural Integrity Principle



Every future feature, system or capability should answer:



1\. Does this strengthen the Creator™?

2\. Does this support Living Creation™?

3\. Does this preserve human meaning?

4\. Does this improve connection and evolution?

5\. Does this respect architectural decisions?



If not, the feature should be reconsidered.
