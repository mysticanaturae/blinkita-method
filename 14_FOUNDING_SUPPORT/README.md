\# Purpose



The BLINKITA FOUNDING SUPPORT™ documentation defines the framework through which BLINKITA seeks financial and strategic support during its foundation and development phase.



This section exists to communicate clearly:



\* what BLINKITA is;

\* what has already been created;

\* what is currently being developed;

\* why dedicated development time is required;

\* what the next development phase will accomplish;

\* what forms of support are possible;

\* what a founding supporter enables;

\* how ownership and creative direction are preserved.



The purpose is not to present BLINKITA as a finished company or finished product.



The purpose is to provide transparent context for individuals and organisations who may wish to support the creation of the system.

\# Why Founding Support Is Needed



The primary limitation at this stage is not the absence of ideas.



The foundation already exists.



The primary requirement is:



\*\*dedicated time.\*\*



BLINKITA is being developed as a founder-led creation in which the conceptual vision, methodology, architecture, documentation and software implementation are being developed as one connected system.



To continue this work systematically requires protected development time.



Founding support therefore exists primarily to create the conditions in which the existing foundation can mature without fragmented development caused by competing financial necessities.



\---



\# Nine-Month Development Principle



The current founding-support model is based on a proposed:



\*\*9-month development runway.\*\*



The purpose of the nine-month period is not to promise that every part of BLINKITA will be completed within that period.



The purpose is to create sufficient continuity for a concentrated development phase.



The intended sequence is:



Foundation Stabilisation



↓



Core System Development



↓



Prototype Evolution



↓



Validation



↓



Demonstration



↓



Future Collaboration

\# Founder Ownership



BLINKITA remains founder-owned.



Founding support does not automatically represent:



\* acquisition of BLINKITA;

\* transfer of intellectual property;

\* transfer of creative direction;

\* control over the methodology;

\* control over the system vision.



The founder remains responsible for the creative and conceptual direction of BLINKITA.



Specific legal, licensing or investment arrangements, if introduced in the future, would require separate agreements.
