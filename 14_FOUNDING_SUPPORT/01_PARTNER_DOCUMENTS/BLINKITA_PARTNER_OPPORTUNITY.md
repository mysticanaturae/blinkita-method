\# The Problem



Creators today have access to more digital tools than ever before.



Yet those tools are usually fragmented.



A creator may have:



\* ideas in one place;

\* documents in another;

\* projects somewhere else;

\* memories in conversations;

\* knowledge in notes;

\* visual material across separate platforms;

\* unfinished creations scattered across years;

\* different identities and audiences across different systems.



The result is often fragmentation.



The creator may continue producing more and more material while gradually losing the ability to see the whole creation.



Important connections disappear.



Previous work becomes difficult to recover.



Projects become isolated.



Creative continuity becomes increasingly difficult to maintain.



The problem is therefore not a lack of digital tools.



It is the lack of \*\*continuity between them and the life of the creator's creation.\*\*
