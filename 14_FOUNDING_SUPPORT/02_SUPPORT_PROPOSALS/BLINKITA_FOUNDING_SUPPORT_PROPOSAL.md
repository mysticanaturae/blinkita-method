\# Development Support Requirement



\## 9-Month Foundation Development Support



The requested development runway is:



\# Nine Months



The purpose of this period is to create a protected and continuous development environment in which the existing BLINKITA foundation can be refined, implemented, tested and prepared for demonstration.



The objective is not to promise that every future component of BLINKITA will be completed within nine months.



The objective is to create a meaningful development cycle in which the current foundation can mature substantially.



\---



\# Why Nine Months?



The current phase requires more than rapid creation.



It requires time for:



\* architectural refinement;

\* implementation;

\* integration;

\* testing;

\* documentation;

\* validation;

\* iteration;

\* reflection;

\* correction;

\* demonstration preparation.



BLINKITA is being developed as a connected system.



Changes in one layer can affect other layers.



A meaningful development period therefore requires continuity rather than fragmented work.



Nine months provides a practical runway for concentrated development while allowing sufficient space for iteration and learning.

\# What Support Does Not Mean



Founding support does not mean:



\* acquisition of BLINKITA;

\* automatic transfer of intellectual property;

\* replacement of the founder;

\* transfer of creative direction;

\* permission to redefine the original vision;

\* premature commercialisation;

\* uncontrolled expansion.



The purpose of support is to enable development.

Any future legal, licensing, investment or partnership arrangements would be defined separately and explicitly.
