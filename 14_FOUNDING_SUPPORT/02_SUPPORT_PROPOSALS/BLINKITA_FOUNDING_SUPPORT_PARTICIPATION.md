\# BLINKITA FOUNDING SUPPORT PARTICIPATION™



\## How Founding Supporters Can Participate in the Emergence of Living Digital Worlds™



\*\*Version:\*\* 1.0

\*\*Status:\*\* Active Development Support Document



\---



\# Purpose



The BLINKITA FOUNDING SUPPORT™ model is designed to create meaningful relationships between BLINKITA and the people or organisations who choose to support its development during the current foundation phase.



Founding support is not intended to be a conventional purchase of a finished product.



It is participation in the development of an emerging system.



A supporter contributes resources that help create:



\* development time;

\* continuity;

\* infrastructure;

\* research;

\* technical development;

\* validation;

\* documentation;

\* demonstration;

\* future collaboration.



In return, BLINKITA seeks to provide supporters with meaningful recognition, access and participation appropriate to their level of support.



\---



\# The Principle



The relationship can be understood simply:



\*\*Support enables development.\*\*



↓



\*\*Development creates BLINKITA.\*\*



↓



\*\*Founding supporters become part of the story of its emergence.\*\*



The purpose is therefore not simply to receive funding.



It is to create an early community of people who can say:



> \*\*I was there when BLINKITA was being built.\*\*



\---



\# What Founding Supporters May Receive



Depending on the level and nature of support, a founding supporter may receive a combination of:



\* BLINKITA membership;

\* Founding Supporter recognition;

\* VIP or Founder-level access;

\* access to selected development experiences;

\* invitations to private demonstrations;

\* early access to selected BLINKITA developments;

\* recognition on appropriate BLINKITA materials;

\* invitations to selected founder events or conversations;

\* opportunities to provide feedback;

\* opportunities for strategic collaboration;

\* access to selected research or development materials;

\* founding supporter certificates or digital recognition;

\* future preferential access to selected BLINKITA experiences or programs.



The exact benefits may evolve as BLINKITA itself develops.



The principle is that early supporters should receive \*\*meaningful early access and recognition\*\*, rather than merely a transactional thank-you.

\# Support Does Not Equal Ownership



Founding support does not automatically provide:



\* equity;

\* shares;

\* ownership of BLINKITA;

\* ownership of BLINKITA METHOD™;

\* ownership of BLINKITA OS™;

\* intellectual property rights;

\* control over the architecture;

\* control over the creative direction;

\* decision-making authority over the project.



The founder retains ownership and creative direction unless a separate written agreement explicitly establishes otherwise.



\---



\# Support Does Not Equal Guaranteed Financial Return



Founding support should not be interpreted as a guaranteed financial investment return.



The current model is intentionally different from a conventional equity investment.



The supporter is primarily enabling development.



Any future investment, equity, licensing, commercial partnership or other financial arrangement would be considered separately and documented through an appropriate formal agreement.

\# What Happens If BLINKITA Evolves?



BLINKITA is an evolving system.



Some benefits, terminology or experiences may therefore change over time.



The purpose of founding participation is not to freeze the future.



Instead, supporters participate at a moment when the system is still being formed.



Where a specific benefit becomes impossible or materially changes, BLINKITA will seek to communicate that clearly and, where appropriate, provide a reasonable alternative.
