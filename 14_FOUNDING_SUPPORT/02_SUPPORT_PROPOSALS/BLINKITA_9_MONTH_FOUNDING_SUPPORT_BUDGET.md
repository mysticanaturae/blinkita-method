\# BLINKITA 9-MONTH FOUNDING SUPPORT BUDGET™



\## Founder Development Runway \& System Development Infrastructure



\*\*Version:\*\* 1.0

\*\*Status:\*\* Development Support Document

\*\*Funding Period:\*\* 9 months

\*\*Funding Target:\*\* USD 33,000



\---



\# Purpose



This document defines the financial requirement for the proposed nine-month BLINKITA founding-support period.



The purpose of the funding is to create the practical conditions required for continued founder-led development of BLINKITA.



The funding is intended to support two interconnected requirements:



1\. \*\*Founder Development Runway\*\*

2\. \*\*Development Infrastructure\*\*



The objective is to create sufficient continuity for the founder to continue developing BLINKITA as one connected system without development being repeatedly interrupted by basic financial and operational constraints.



\---



\# Funding Target



\## Total Nine-Month Requirement



\# \*\*USD 33,000\*\*



This represents an intended development runway of approximately:



\# \*\*USD 3,667 per month\*\*



The amount is designed as a modest development-support budget rather than a high-growth operating budget.



It is intended to provide stability rather than luxury.

\# Monthly Development Principle

\## USD 3,667
