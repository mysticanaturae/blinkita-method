\* documentation;

\* research;

\*\*BLINKITA 9-MONTH FOUNDING SUPPORT BUDGET™\*\*

\# Funding Requirement



\## Nine-Month Founder Development Runway



The proposed funding requirement is:



\# \*\*USD 33,000\*\*



The purpose of this amount is to create sufficient continuity for a concentrated nine-month development period.



The funding is intended to support two interconnected categories.



\---



\## 1. Founder Development \& Sustainable Working Conditions



The founder is currently integrating:



\* creative vision;

\* methodology;

\* system architecture;

\* documentation;

\* research;

\* software development;

\* prototype development;

\* system integration;

\* validation.



The development therefore depends substantially on the founder's available time and ability to work consistently.



The runway is intended to create sustainable working conditions in which this work can continue without being repeatedly interrupted by competing financial and practical demands.



This is not intended as a luxury or lifestyle budget.



It is intended to establish the basic stability required for sustained founder-led development.



\---



\## 2. Development Infrastructure \& External Requirements



Funding may additionally support:



\* AI and software tools;

\* hosting;

\* storage;

\* domains;

\* email and communication services;

\* development services;

\* testing environments;

\* deployment;

\* development equipment;

\* specialised technical services;

\* independent technical review;

\* security assessment;

\* documentation and demonstration materials.



The detailed allocation is maintained separately in:



\*\*BLINKITA 9-MONTH FOUNDING SUPPORT BUDGET™\*\*



The allocation may evolve as actual development requirements become clearer.

\# Success Criteria

\### Experience

\# What This Roadmap Does Not Promise



This roadmap does not claim that:



\* the entire BLINKITA OS™ will be completed within nine months;

\* every conceptual system will be implemented;

\* all technical risks will be eliminated;

\* the final commercial model is already determined;

\* the long-term ecosystem can be predicted with certainty.



The roadmap represents a focused development direction.



Implementation may reveal new requirements.



Responsible adaptation is therefore part of the development process.
