\# Introduction



This document answers common questions that may arise when considering financial or strategic support for BLINKITA.

\# 8A. How was the USD 33,000 funding target determined?



The proposed funding target is based on a nine-month founder development runway.



The total requirement is:



\*\*USD 33,000\*\*



The intended allocation is:



\* \*\*USD 27,000\*\* — Founder \& Family Development Runway;

\* \*\*USD 2,500\*\* — Software, AI \& Development Infrastructure;

\* \*\*USD 1,500\*\* — Hardware \& Development Equipment Reserve;

\* \*\*USD 1,000\*\* — Independent Technical / Specialist Support;

\* \*\*USD 1,000\*\* — Contingency \& Unexpected Development Costs.



The budget is designed to provide sufficient continuity for a concentrated nine-month development period rather than to create unnecessary organisational overhead.

\# 8B. Why does the budget include founder and family living costs?



Because BLINKITA is currently a founder-led development project.



The founder is directly responsible for integrating:



\* conceptual development;

\* methodology;

\* architecture;

\* software development;

\* documentation;

\* research;

\* testing;

\* validation;

Its purpose is to create the stability required for sustained development.

\# 8D. Will all USD 33,000 necessarily be spent?



Not necessarily.
