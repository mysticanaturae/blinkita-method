\# BLINKITA METHOD™

\# GOVERNANCE OF THE METHOD



Version: 0.2



Status: Active Governance Framework



\---



\# Purpose



This document defines how BLINKITA METHOD™ evolves while preserving its identity, coherence and integrity.



The purpose of governance is not to restrict creativity.



The purpose of governance is to ensure that evolution remains connected to the original principles of the methodology.



\---



\# Governance Principle



A Living Method™ must be able to evolve.



A Living Method™ must also remember what makes it itself.



Therefore:



Evolution



Memory



Responsibility



=



Continuity





\---



\# Scope



Governance applies to:



\- Canonical concepts;

\- terminology;

\- frameworks;

\- blueprints;

\- standards;

\- playbooks;

\- architecture documents;

\- research interpretations.



\---



\# Governance Layers



BLINKITA METHOD™ evolution is governed through:





Foundation Protection



↓



Concept Governance



↓



Document Governance



↓



Implementation Alignment



↓



Research Feedback





\---



\# 1. Foundation Protection



The Canon represents the deepest identity layer of BLINKITA METHOD™.



Changes affecting Canonical Principles require the highest level of review.



The Canon should evolve only when:



\- new understanding emerges;

\- contradictions are discovered;

\- research provides meaningful insight;

\- evolution strengthens rather than replaces the foundation.



\---



\# 2. Concept Governance



New canonical concepts require:



\## Definition



A clear explanation of meaning.



\---



\## Classification



The concept must be identified as:



\- philosophical;

\- methodological;

\- architectural;

\- technical;

\- experiential.



\---



\## Relationship Mapping



The concept must define:



\- parent concepts;

\- related concepts;

\- dependent concepts.



\---



\## Source Document



Every canonical concept requires an owning document.



\---



\## Glossary Entry



No concept becomes canonical without glossary registration.



\---

\# 3. Document Governance



Every important document should contain:



\- title;

\- version;

\- status;

\- purpose;

\- relationship to other documents.



\---



\# Document Status



\## Draft



Early exploration.



\---



\## Proposed



Structured idea awaiting wider review.



\---



\## Active



Currently used within the methodology.



\---



\## Canonical



Protected reference document.



\---



\## Deprecated



No longer recommended but preserved for history.

\---



\# Impact Review



Before accepting a significant change, evaluate:



\## Concept Impact



Does it change meaning?



\---



\## Terminology Impact



Does it affect definitions?



\---



\## Architecture Impact



Does it affect relationships between systems?



\---



\## Implementation Impact



Does it affect BLINKITA OS™ or other implementations?



\---



\## Research Impact



Does it change assumptions or validation paths?

\- challenge assumptions.

External feedback does not automatically become methodology truth.



It enters through the governance process.

\# 10. Founder and Creator Relationship



BLINKITA METHOD™ originated through the experience and development journey of its Creator™.



Future evolution should preserve:



\- original intention;

\- core principles;

\- continuity of meaning.

\---



\# Governance Responsibility Model



BLINKITA METHOD™ governance separates contribution, review and approval.



\## Concept Contributor



May:



\- propose new concepts;

\- document observations;

\- suggest improvements.



A proposal does not automatically become part of the methodology.



\---



\## Method Reviewer



Reviews:



\- conceptual coherence;

\- terminology consistency;

\- relationship with existing architecture;

\- potential conflicts.



\---



\## Canonical Approval



A concept becomes Canonical only after:



\- definition exists;

\- glossary entry exists;

\- relationships are documented;

\- impact review is completed;

\- governance decision is recorded.



\---



\## Research Contribution



Researchers and external reviewers may provide:



\- observations;

\- evidence;

\- criticism;

\- alternative interpretations.



Research informs evolution but does not independently modify the Canon.



\---
