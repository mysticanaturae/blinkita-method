\# BLINKITA METHOD™

\# DOCUMENT MATURITY STANDARD



Version: 0.1



Status: Active Standard



\---



\# Purpose



This standard defines how BLINKITA METHOD™ documents communicate their development maturity.



Because BLINKITA METHOD™ is an evolving methodology, documents may exist in different stages of development.



Document maturity allows creators, reviewers and collaborators to understand:



\- current confidence level;

\- intended usage;

\- development status;

\- review requirements.



\---



\# Principle



A document status describes the maturity of the document.



It does not describe the value of the idea.



An experimental document may contain important future concepts.



A canonical document may still evolve through governance.



\---



\# Required Metadata



Each major methodology document SHOULD contain:



Title



Version



Status



Last Updated



Purpose





\---



\# Maturity Status Levels



\---



\# 1. Draft



Meaning:



Early development version.



Characteristics:



\- incomplete;

\- open to structural changes;

\- requires further development.



Usage:



Internal exploration.



\---



\# 2. Proposed



Meaning:



A developed idea suggested for inclusion.



Characteristics:



\- defined concept;

\- requires review;

\- relationship mapping may be incomplete.



Usage:



Future consideration.



\---



\# 3. Experimental



Meaning:



A concept or structure being tested.



Characteristics:



\- active exploration;

\- learning expected;

\- conclusions not final.



Usage:



Research and experimentation.



\---



\# 4. Active



Meaning:



Currently used within the methodology.



Characteristics:



\- defined;

\- documented;

\- available for application.



Usage:



Current methodology development.



\---



\# 5. Canonical



Meaning:



A protected core element.



Characteristics:



\- aligned with Canon;

\- governed;

\- stable meaning.



Usage:



Foundation of the methodology.



\---



\# 6. Under Validation



Meaning:



An element being evaluated through application or research.



Characteristics:



\- used in practice;

\- evidence collection active.



Usage:



Research phase.



\---



\# 7. Validated



Meaning:



An element supported by sufficient evidence.



Characteristics:



\- documented application;

\- reviewed outcomes;

\- defined boundaries.



Usage:



Recognised methodology component.



\---



\# 8. Deprecated



Meaning:



A previous element no longer actively used.



Characteristics:



\- preserved for history;

\- replaced or transformed.



Usage:



Living Memory™ archive.



\---



\# Status Progression



Documents may evolve through:





Draft



↓



Proposed



↓



Experimental



↓



Active



↓



Under Validation



↓



Canonical / Validated





Not every document must follow every stage.



\---



\# Governance Rules



Changing document maturity requires:



\- understanding impact;

\- updating relationships;

\- preserving history;

\- recording significant changes.



\---



\# Relationship With Other Layers



Document maturity supports:



\## Governance



By showing what may change.



\## Glossary



By showing concept maturity.



\## Standards



By showing requirement stability.



\## Research



By showing evidence status.



\---



\# Recommended Future Metadata



Future versions may include:



\- owner;

\- review date;

\- related concepts;

\- dependencies;

\- implementation status;

\- research references.



\---



\# Final Principle



A living methodology does not hide uncertainty.



It organizes uncertainty.



Document maturity makes evolution visible.
