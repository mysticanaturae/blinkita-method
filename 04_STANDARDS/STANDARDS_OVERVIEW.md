\# Purpose



This document defines the role, structure and governance of standards within BLINKITA METHOD™.



Standards exist to protect the integrity, quality and coherence of the methodology.



\---



\# What Is A Standard?



A Standard defines qualities, requirements and boundaries that should be preserved when applying BLINKITA METHOD™.



A Standard is not:



\- a creative limitation;

\- a fixed template;

\- a replacement for vision.



A Standard is:



\- a quality protection mechanism;

\- a consistency framework;

\- a reference for evaluation.

\# Standard Requirement Language



Standards use controlled requirement language:



\---



\## Must



A required condition.



Failure means the standard is not satisfied.



\---



\## Should



A recommended condition.



Deviation requires explanation.



\---



\## May



An optional possibility.

\# Compliance Levels



A component may be evaluated as:



\## Not Evaluated



No assessment performed.



\---



\## Emerging



Early application exists.



\---



\## Applied



Standard is actively considered.



\---



\## Conforming



Requirements are satisfied.



\---



\## Evolving



The standard itself is being improved through learning.



\---



\# Exceptions



Exceptions are allowed when:



\- the reason is documented;

\- the impact is understood;

\- the deviation creates meaningful value.



Exceptions should not silently redefine the standard.
