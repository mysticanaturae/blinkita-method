\# BLINKITA METHOD™



\# EXPERIENCE STANDARD™



\## Principles for Designing Meaningful Living Experiences™



Partner \& Validation Perspective



\---



\# Purpose of This Standard



The Experience Standard™ defines the principles that guide how people encounter, explore and participate in a Living World™.



Within BLINKITA METHOD™, experience is not considered a collection of isolated interactions.



It is the continuous relationship between a participant and an evolving Living World™.



This standard provides criteria for designing, evaluating and improving meaningful experiences that remain authentic throughout the world's evolution.



\---



\# Core Principle



People may enter a Living World™ through a single interaction.



They remain because of the experience they continuously create together with the world.



Experience is not delivered.



Experience is lived.



\---



\# Experience Principles



\## 1. Meaning Before Interaction



Every interaction should serve a meaningful purpose.



Technology and interface support the experience but never replace meaning.



\---



\## 2. Participation



Participants should be encouraged to contribute rather than simply consume.



A Living World™ grows through shared participation.



\---



\## 3. Presence



Every interaction should strengthen the feeling of entering a coherent living reality.



Experiences should create immersion rather than distraction.



\---



\## 4. Progression



Experiences should unfold naturally over time.



Each step should create curiosity for the next stage of the journey.



\---



\## 5. Personal Relevance



While a Living World™ serves many participants, each person should be able to discover a unique relationship with it.



Meaning emerges through personal experience.



\---



\## 6. Continuity



Experiences should remain connected across time.



Each interaction becomes part of the participant's ongoing relationship with the Living World™.



\---



\## 7. Evolution



Experiences should evolve together with the Living World™.



New possibilities may emerge without disrupting coherence or identity.



\---



\# Evaluation Questions



Experience quality may be explored through questions such as:



\- Does every interaction support the purpose of the Living World™?

\- Does the experience invite meaningful participation?

\- Is progression intuitive and engaging?

\- Can participants build long-term relationships with the world?

\- Does the experience remain coherent as the world evolves?



\---



\# Relationship With Other Standards



The Experience Standard™ supports:



\- Design Standard™

\- Story Standard™

\- Technology Standard™

\- Evolution Standard™



It also provides practical guidance for Experience Architecture Blueprint™, Living Design Architecture Blueprint™ and World Activation Blueprint™.



\---



\# Validation Perspective



This standard enables external reviewers to evaluate whether a Living World™ creates meaningful participation instead of isolated user interactions.



Experience quality becomes observable through engagement, continuity and long-term relationship building.



\---



\# Research Connection



Research questions include:



\- Which experiences create lasting engagement?

\- How do people develop relationships with Living Worlds™?

\- What makes participation meaningful over time?

\- How can experiences evolve while remaining coherent?



\---



\# Final Statement



A Living World™ is not remembered because people used it.



It is remembered because people experienced becoming part of it.



The Experience Standard™ provides the principles that transform interaction into meaningful participation and lasting connection.
