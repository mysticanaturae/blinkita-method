\# Introduction



The Academy Framework™ defines how knowledge, methodologies and experiences created through BLINKITA METHOD™ are transformed into learning pathways for future creators.



A Living Method™ does not only preserve knowledge.



It teaches others how to understand, apply and evolve that knowledge.



\---



\# Purpose of This Framework



The purpose of the Academy Framework™ is to establish a learning ecosystem that supports:



\- knowledge transmission

\- creator development

\- methodological understanding

\- practical application

\- continuous evolution



\---



\# Core Principle



Learning within BLINKITA METHOD™ is not based only on information transfer.



It is based on transformation.



The purpose is not only:



"What do you know?"



but also:



"Who do you become through what you create?"
