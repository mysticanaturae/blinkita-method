\# Blueprint Architecture



\## 1. Vision Architecture



Purpose:



Defines the original possibility, intention and future direction of a Living World™.



Key questions:



\- Why should this world exist?

\- What future possibility does it represent?

\- What change does it seek to create?



Related documents:



\- Vision Blueprint™

\- Story Blueprint™
