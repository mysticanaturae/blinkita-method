\# BLINKITA METHOD™



\# CANON



\## Core Definition



BLINKITA METHOD™ is an evolving methodology for creating Living Worlds™.



The Canon defines the essential principles, concepts and terminology that form the identity of the methodology.



It represents the elements that should remain consistent throughout future development.



\---



\# Core Concepts



\## Living World™



A Living World™ is a creation designed as an evolving system.



It contains:



\- identity

\- purpose

\- structure

\- experience

\- connection

\- evolution



A Living World™ is not only created.



It is developed, observed and continuously refined.



\---



\## World Builder™



A World Builder™ is a creator who designs and develops meaningful living systems.



The role combines:



\- vision

\- creativity

\- architecture

\- responsibility

\- evolution



A World Builder™ transforms ideas into environments where possibilities can grow.



\---



\## Creator Journey™



The Creator Journey™ describes the evolution of an idea from first inspiration to living implementation.



The journey follows:



Vision



↓



Identity



↓



Architecture



↓



Creation



↓



Experience



↓



Evolution

\## Living Systems Principle



BLINKITA METHOD™ treats creations as systems that can evolve.



A living system requires:



\- purpose

\- memory

\- relationships

\- adaptation

\- growth
