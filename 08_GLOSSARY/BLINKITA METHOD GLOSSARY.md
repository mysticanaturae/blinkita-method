\# Purpose



This glossary defines the controlled vocabulary of BLINKITA METHOD™.



Its purpose is to preserve conceptual clarity across:



\- methodology;

\- documentation;

\- software architecture;

\- education;

\- research;

\- case studies.



\---



\# Alias Governance



Aliases may exist to improve accessibility, translation and communication.



However, aliases must not create conceptual ambiguity.



Each canonical term may define:



\## Allowed Aliases



Alternative expressions that preserve the intended meaning.



Examples:



Living World™



Allowed:



\* evolving world;

\* living creation system;

\* creative ecosystem.



These expressions may be used for explanation.



They do not replace the canonical term.



\---



\## Restricted Aliases



Expressions that may create confusion and should be avoided.



Examples:



Living World™



Avoid:



\* project;

\* product;

\* simple application;

\* static platform.



Reason:



These terms reduce the concept into a narrower category and remove the evolutionary dimension.



\---



\## Deprecated Aliases



Previous terms that should no longer be used as primary terminology.



No deprecated aliases currently registered.



\---



\# Concept Ownership Principle



Every canonical concept has:



\* primary definition;

\* source document;

\* relationship network;

\* responsible evolution path.



The Glossary is the central reference point for terminology consistency.



\---



\# Concept Change Rule



When a concept changes meaning:



The change must include:



\* previous definition;

\* new definition;

\* reason for change;

\* affected documents;

\* affected implementations;

\* governance review.



A concept may evolve.



Its history must remain preserved.
