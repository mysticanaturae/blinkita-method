\# Introduction



The World Legacy Playbook™ defines how a Living World™ preserves its knowledge, identity, experiences and contributions beyond individual moments.



A Living World™ is not only measured by what it creates today.



It is also measured by what remains available for future discovery, learning and evolution.



Legacy creates continuity between the past, present and future.



\---



\# Purpose of This Playbook



The purpose of the World Legacy Playbook™ is to define systems that preserve the value created throughout the lifecycle of a Living World™.



It helps answer:



\- What knowledge should be preserved?

\- How can experiences become wisdom?

\- How can future participants understand the origin of the world?

\- How can the world continue beyond its original creators?



\---



\# Core Principle



Legacy is not simply documentation of the past.



Legacy is living knowledge that continues creating value in the future.



A Living World™ does not preserve itself by remaining unchanged.



It preserves itself by allowing its essence, wisdom and meaning to continue evolving.
