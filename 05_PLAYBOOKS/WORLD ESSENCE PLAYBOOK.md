\# Introduction



The World Essence Playbook™ defines the process of discovering the fundamental essence of a Living World™.



Every Living World™ contains a unique core that gives meaning, direction and coherence to everything that emerges from it.



This essence is not artificially created.



It is discovered, articulated and protected throughout the evolution of the world.



\---



\# Purpose of This Playbook



The purpose of the World Essence Playbook™ is to guide creators through the process of identifying the deepest foundation of a Living World™.



It helps answer:



\- What is the fundamental truth of this world?

\- Why does this world need to exist?

\- What transformation does it carry?

\- What must always remain authentic?

\- What makes this world unique?



\---

A Living World™ is not defined only by what it creates.

\- identity
