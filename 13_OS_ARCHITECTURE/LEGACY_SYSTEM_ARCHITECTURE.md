\# Introduction



Legacy System Architecture™ defines how creations, knowledge, experiences and contributions continue beyond their original moment of creation.



A Living World™ is not measured only by what it creates today.



It is measured by what it allows future generations to discover, learn and build upon.

\# Purpose



The purpose of Legacy System Architecture™ is to preserve meaningful contributions while allowing them to continue evolving through time.



It supports:



\- continuity

\- preservation

\- contribution

\- intergenerational connection

\- future creation

\# Core Principle



Legacy is not about preserving the past unchanged.
