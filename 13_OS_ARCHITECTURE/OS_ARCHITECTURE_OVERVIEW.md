\# BLINKITA OS™



\# OS ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW™



\## The Architecture of a Living Creation Operating System™



Version 1.0



\---



\# Introduction



BLINKITA OS™ is a Living Creation Operating System™ designed to support the creation, evolution and connection of Living Worlds™.



It is not designed to manage projects.



It is designed to help Living Creations™ live, evolve and connect.



Within BLINKITA OS™, the central entity is not the project.



The central entity is the Creator™.



Projects are temporary expressions.



Living Worlds™ are evolving environments.



Creations are meaningful expressions of human imagination, intention and contribution.



\---



\# Purpose of BLINKITA OS™



The purpose of BLINKITA OS™ is to provide a digital habitat where creators can:



\- understand their creative identity

\- organize their creations

\- develop Living Worlds™

\- preserve creative memory

\- navigate evolution

\- connect with other creators

\- contribute to a larger Living Civilization™



\---



\# Core Principle



Traditional digital systems are designed around management.



BLINKITA OS™ is designed around evolution.



Traditional systems ask:



"What tasks need to be completed?"



BLINKITA OS™ asks:



"What is becoming alive?"
