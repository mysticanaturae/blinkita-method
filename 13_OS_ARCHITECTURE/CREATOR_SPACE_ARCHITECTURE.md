\# Introduction



Creator Space™ is the personal living environment of a Creator™ within BLINKITA OS™.



It is not a user profile.



It is not a dashboard.



It is not a collection of settings.



Creator Space™ is the place where a Creator™ discovers, develops, organizes and evolves their creative identity and Living Creations™.



\---



\# Purpose of Creator Space™



The purpose of Creator Space™ is to provide every Creator™ with a dedicated environment where they can:



\- understand who they are as creators

\- define their vision

\- organize their Living Worlds™

\- develop creations

\- preserve creative memory

\- navigate personal evolution

\- connect with other creators



\---



\# Core Principle



A Creator™ is not managed by the system.



A Creator™ is supported by the system.



BLINKITA OS™ exists to amplify creative awareness, not replace human intention.
