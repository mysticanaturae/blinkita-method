Governance System Architecture™ defines how BLINKITA OS™ preserves integrity, responsibility and alignment throughout the evolution of Living Worlds™.

The purpose of Governance System Architecture™ is to define the principles that guide responsible evolution within BLINKITA OS™.

\- long-term sustainability
