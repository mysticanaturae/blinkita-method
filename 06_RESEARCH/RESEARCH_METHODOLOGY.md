\# BLINKITA METHOD™



\# RESEARCH METHODOLOGY



\## A Practice-Based Research Approach



\---



\# Purpose of This Document



This document describes the research approach used during the development of BLINKITA METHOD™ and BLINKITA OS™.



The purpose is to explain how observations, insights, frameworks and improvements emerge through the creation process.



\---



\# Research Approach



BLINKITA research follows a practice-based methodology.



The system is not developed only through theoretical analysis.



It is developed through:



Creation



↓



Observation



↓



Documentation



↓



Reflection



↓



Improvement



↓



New Creation



\---



\# The Creator as Research Participant



The first research participant is the creator of BLINKITA.



The creator is simultaneously:



\- the person experiencing the problem

\- the designer of the solution

\- the first user

\- the observer of the process

\- the documenter of discoveries



This creates a direct connection between problem, solution and practical experience.



\---



\# Genesis Case Study Method



BLINKITA itself functions as the Genesis Case Study.



The methodology is developed by observing:



\- what challenges appear

\- what systems are needed

\- what structures improve clarity

\- what approaches create friction

\- what solutions create better continuity



\---



\# Documentation Method



The research process preserves:



\- decisions

\- assumptions

\- experiments

\- revisions

\- failures

\- discoveries

\- emerging concepts



Documentation is considered part of the research itself.



\---



\# Iterative Development



The methodology evolves through cycles.



A concept may move through:



Idea



↓



Exploration



↓



Framework



↓



Blueprint



↓



Implementation



↓



Observation



↓



Evolution



\---



\# Qualitative Research Elements



The research includes observation of:



\- creator experience

\- workflow improvements

\- organizational clarity

\- creative continuity

\- reduction of friction

\- ability to transform ideas into reality



\---



\# AI Collaboration as a Research Element



AI assistance is included as part of the exploration.



The research investigates how AI may support creators through:



\- structured thinking

\- documentation

\- learning acceleration

\- system design assistance

\- reflection and iteration



The creator remains the source of vision, intention and final decisions.



\---



\# Research Limitations



This research begins from a single primary case study.



Future validation requires:



\- additional creators

\- independent projects

\- different creative fields

\- long-term observation



\---



\# Research Goal



The goal is not only to create a system.



The goal is to understand whether a structured Living World™ approach can help creators preserve, develop and evolve meaningful creations over time.
