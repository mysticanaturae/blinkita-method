\# BLINKITA METHOD™



\# DEVELOPMENT ROADMAP



\## Evolution Path of a Living Methodology™



\---



\# Purpose



This document describes the long-term evolution path of the BLINKITA METHOD™.



The roadmap does not represent a fixed production schedule.



It represents the research and development direction through which the methodology evolves, is tested and refined.



\---



\# Development Philosophy



BLINKITA METHOD™ is developed as a Living Methodology™.

↓

↓



Implementation



↓

↓

↓



Evolution





Each stage creates knowledge that supports the next stage of development.
