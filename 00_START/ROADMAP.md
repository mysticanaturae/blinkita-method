\# BLINKITA METHOD™



\# ROADMAP™



\*\*Development Evolution Path\*\*



\---



\# Purpose



This roadmap documents the planned evolution of BLINKITA METHOD™.



It is not a fixed project schedule.



It represents the direction of research, development and refinement as the methodology evolves through practical implementation.



\---



\# Development Philosophy



BLINKITA METHOD™ develops through evolutionary layers.



The process follows:





Vision



↓



Foundation



↓



Framework



↓



Blueprint



↓



Standard



↓



Playbook



↓



Implementation



↓



Observation



↓



Research



↓



Validation



↓



Evolution



↓



Transmission





Each stage builds upon previous knowledge.
