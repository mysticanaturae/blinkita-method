\# BLINKITA METHOD™



\# CONTRIBUTING



\## Research \& Development Collaboration Guide



\---



\# Purpose



This document defines how future contributors, researchers and collaborators may participate in the evolution of the BLINKITA METHOD™.



The methodology is developed as a Living System™.



Contributions are not only additions of content.



They are part of an ongoing process of observation, refinement and evolution.



\---



\# Contribution Philosophy



BLINKITA METHOD™ evolves through:



\- exploration

\- research

\- experimentation

\- documentation

\- reflection

\- improvement



Every contribution should support the coherence and integrity of the methodology.



\---



\# Types of Contributions



Future contributions may include:



\## Research Contributions



Examples:



\- observations

\- hypotheses

\- experiments

\- case study analysis

\- research insights



\---



\## Methodology Contributions



Examples:



\- framework improvements

\- blueprint refinement

\- playbook development

\- standards evolution



\---



\## Documentation Contributions



Examples:



\- terminology clarification

\- structural improvements

\- examples

\- additional explanations



\---



\# Contribution Principles



\## 1. Preserve the Foundation



New contributions should respect:



\- core principles

\- methodology philosophy

\- established terminology

\- architectural relationships



\---



\## 2. Document Evolution



Changes should be recorded.



The history of development is part of the research value.



\---



\## 3. Add Meaningful Value



Contributions should improve:



\- clarity

\- usability

\- understanding

\- application potential



\---



\## 4. Maintain Coherence



New concepts should connect with the existing architecture.



A Living Methodology™ grows through relationships, not isolated additions.



\---



\# Review Process



Future contributions should consider:



1\. Purpose



Why does this addition exist?



2\. Connection



How does it relate to existing layers?



3\. Impact



How does it improve the methodology?



4\. Evolution



What new possibility does it create?



\---



\# Collaboration Models



Future collaboration may include:



\- research partnerships

\- case study contributions

\- educational collaboration

\- methodology testing

\- independent applications



\---



\# Current Development Status



BLINKITA METHOD™ is currently an evolving research project.



External contributions will become increasingly relevant as the methodology reaches validation and application stages.



\---



\# Final Statement



Contributing to BLINKITA METHOD™ means participating in the evolution of a methodology designed to help transform ideas into meaningful living systems.



The goal is not only to add information.



The goal is to grow understanding.
